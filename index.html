How to use

Each dot is a podcast.
• Size = how often it's recommended by others.
• Color = the podcast's hosting company.
• Zoom in close with the mouse wheel and the dots turn into the actual cover art.
• Click any podcast to focus on it: outgoing arrows (cyan) show what it recommends, incoming (pink) show who recommends it.
• Search a title above to pan and zoom to it.
• Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, Esc to exit focus.
About this app
This is a static web app. All the rendering, search and layout happens locally in your browser. The dataset is fetched once from podcastindex.org and cached in your local machine for 24 hours. Layout and frame rate depend on your machine (see Frames per Second, FPS below): a fast laptop will fly through the node graph, while an older one will feel it. Built for desktop browsers, not optimized for mobile or touch.
